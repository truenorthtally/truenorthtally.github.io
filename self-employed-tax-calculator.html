How this is calculated

Uses the published 2026 federal and provincial tax brackets and basic personal amounts, and 2026 CPP (11.9% on earnings between $3,500 and $74,600, plus 8% CPP2 up to $85,000). Self-employed people pay both halves of CPP. The "employer" half and the enhanced parts are deducted from income, and the base employee part is claimed as a credit. Ontario surtax and Health Premium are included. It assumes self-employment is your only income and leaves out other credits (tuition, RRSP, dependants and so on), so treat it as a planning estimate, not a tax return. Check canada.ca or a tax professional before filing.

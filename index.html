Questions

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. In Google Drive choose New → File upload, then open the file with Google Sheets. It also works in Excel (desktop or web) and Apple Numbers. There are no macros.

Is this tax advice?

No. It's an organising tool that follows the CRA Form T2125 layout. Rules change and everyone's situation differs, so check canada.ca or a tax professional before you file.

Do I need to be registered for GST/HST?

Many small businesses don't have to register until revenue passes $30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters, but some activities (such as ride-sharing) have different rules. Confirm on canada.ca. The workbook works either way: set "Registered?" to Yes or No.

Which provinces are supported?

All 13 provinces and territories. HST and GST rates are built in and editable. Provincial PST/QST is outside the GST/HST calculations.
